Read this before you compare: Apple MPP broke open rates

Since 2021, Apple Mail Privacy Protection pre-loads tracking pixels through proxy servers, recording an "open" even when nobody read the email. That is why open rates roughly doubled against the last clean pre-MPP baseline — Campaign Monitor's 2021 figures of 21.5% open, 2.3% click and 10.5% click-to-open — while click rates barely moved.

The rule: compare opens only within the same platform and against your own history. For judging whether an email worked, use click rate, replies and conversions — those require a real human action. Click-to-open rate is also distorted, because its denominator (opens) is inflated.

Deliverability guardrails

  • Bounce rate: investigate above roughly 2% — that usually signals a list-quality problem, not a content problem.
  • Spam complaints: Gmail guidance for bulk senders is to keep reported spam rates below 0.3%, and to treat 0.1% as the working target.
  • Unsubscribes rising after a frequency change is information, not failure — especially since Google's February 2024 one-click unsubscribe requirement made leaving a list much easier for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good email open rate in 2025?

The median open rate across all industries was 43.46% in MailerLite's 2025 dataset, but open rates are inflated by Apple Mail Privacy Protection. Compare your open rate within the same platform and over time, and treat click rate as the more reliable benchmark.

What is a good email click rate?

The median click rate was 2.09% in MailerLite's 2025 data and 2.62% in Mailchimp's dataset. Click rate is the most trustworthy engagement metric because it requires a real human action and is not distorted by Apple Mail Privacy Protection.

Why did unsubscribe rates rise in 2025?

MailerLite's median unsubscribe rate more than doubled year over year, from 0.08% in 2024 to 0.22% in 2025. A major driver is Google's February 2024 requirement that bulk senders support one-click unsubscribe, which made leaving a list dramatically easier.

Why do email benchmarks disagree across sources?

Different platforms serve different sender mixes (newsletters and nonprofits vs ecommerce promos), report medians vs means, and handle Apple Mail Privacy Protection differently. Always compare your numbers within the same platform and against your own history first.
