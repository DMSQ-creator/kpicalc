Weekly Data Point

One number every week, with its source and date. Each entry is a single published figure — not an estimate, not a trend piece. If a week has no good public source, that week stays empty. That rule is the whole point.

Week of November 23, 2026

5.13%average website conversion rate across 13 industries

Across 110 million+ sessions and 5 million+ conversions tracked through Ruler's platform, the average website turns 5.13% of sessions into a qualified lead or a sale — counting form fills and phone calls. Software sites sit well above the average at 7.6%; travel is lowest at 1.9%.

Source: Ruler Analytics, Conversion Rate Benchmarks for 2027 (published November 13, 2026) — www.ruleranalytics.com

Week of November 16, 2026

3.04%median annual churn for software businesses

Across the subscription businesses billing through Recurly's network, the median software business loses 3.04% of subscribers a year; top-quartile performers hold churn at 1.78% or below. Recurly puts the range where most well-run subscription businesses operate at 2-4% a year.

Source: Recurly, Churn Rate Benchmarks (published July 29, 2026) — recurly.com

Week of November 9, 2026

57%of SaaS products use a free trial as their primary entry point

In ChartMogul's January 2026 survey of 200 B2B software products, 57% named a free trial as the primary way new customers start — more than twice the 26% that lead with freemium. Only 7% use a reverse trial.

Source: ChartMogul, SaaS Conversion Report (February 2026) — chartmogul.com

Week of November 2, 2026

$66.69average cost per lead in search ads

Across over 13,000 US search campaigns on Google and Microsoft Ads, April 2025 to March 2026. It is the first year-over-year fall in cost per lead in five years, driven by conversion rates rising in 87% of industries — not by cheaper clicks, which still averaged $5.42.

Source: WordStream by LocaliQ, Google Ads Benchmarks 2026 (published May 2026) — www.wordstream.com

Week of October 27, 2026

8%median free-to-paid conversion

Across 200 SaaS products surveyed by ChartMogul in January 2026. "Good" free-trial conversion is 4–6%; trials that require a credit card convert at 30% — the card requirement filters for intent before the trial even starts.

Source: ChartMogul, January 2026 survey (200 products) — as documented in Digital Applied

How this column works

  • One figure per entry. A number, what it measures, and who published it — nothing more.
  • Source and date on every entry. No figure appears without a named publisher and a publication date.
  • Empty weeks stay empty. If no solid public source turns up in a given week, we skip that week rather than filling it with an estimate.
  • Separate from the benchmark reports. The annual reports (email, SaaS churn, CAC & LTV) are reviewed every October; this column is the weekly pulse between those reviews.

How to cite

Quote any figure with credit to KPICalc Benchmarks and a link back to this page. For the underlying data, cite the original publisher named in the entry.
