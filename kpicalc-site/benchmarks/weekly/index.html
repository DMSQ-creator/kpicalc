One number every week, with its source and date. Each entry is a single published figure — not an estimate, not a trend piece. If a week has no good public source, that week stays empty. That rule is the whole point.

Week of October 27, 2026

8%median free-to-paid conversion

Across 200 SaaS products surveyed by ChartMogul in January 2026. "Good" free-trial conversion is 4–6%; trials that require a credit card convert at 30% — the card requirement filters for intent before the trial even starts.

Source: ChartMogul, January 2026 survey (200 products) — as documented in Digital Applied

How this column works

  • One figure per entry. A number, what it measures, and who published it — nothing more.
  • Source and date on every entry. No figure appears without a named publisher and a publication date.
  • Empty weeks stay empty. If no solid public source turns up in a given week, we skip that week rather than filling it with an estimate.
  • Separate from the benchmark reports. The annual reports (email, SaaS churn, CAC & LTV) are reviewed every October; this column is the weekly pulse between those reviews.

How to cite

Quote any figure with credit to KPICalc Benchmarks and a link back to this page. For the underlying data, cite the original publisher named in the entry.
