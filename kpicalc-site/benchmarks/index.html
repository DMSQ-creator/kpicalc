Original, dated data reports for marketers — email performance, SaaS churn, CAC payback and more. Every number sourced, every report updated on a published schedule.

How we build each report

  1. Sources first. Every figure comes from a named public source — vendor benchmark publications, public datasets, or aggregated data from our own calculators where noted.
  2. Dates on everything. Each number carries the period it covers. A 2023 figure never poses as a current one.
  3. Published cadence. Each report states how often it is refreshed (usually annually) and shows its last-updated date.
  4. No silent estimates. If a figure cannot be sourced, we say so — we do not fill gaps with invented numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Where does KPICalc Benchmarks data come from?

Every report lists its sources: public datasets, vendor benchmark publications, and aggregated figures from our own calculators where noted. No number is published without a source and a date. If we cannot source a figure, we say so instead of estimating silently.

How often are benchmark reports updated?

Each report states its own update cadence on the page — most are refreshed annually. The publication date and last-updated date are always shown at the top of the report.

Can I cite KPICalc Benchmarks in my own article?

Yes. The only attribution we ask for is a link back to the report page. You are welcome to quote figures with credit to KPICalc Benchmarks.
